# DepthWizard

One optical satellite or aerial image becomes a digital surface model and a navigable 3D scene.
Built for Smart India Hackathon 2026, problem statement 26175 (ISRO).

Upload a GeoTIFF for heights in metres, or a PNG/JPG for a relative model. Sample scenes are
included. Source: https://github.com/yats0x7/DepthWizard

**This Space runs on free CPU**, so a scene takes minutes rather than the seconds it takes on a
GPU, and job outputs are cleared whenever the Space restarts.
