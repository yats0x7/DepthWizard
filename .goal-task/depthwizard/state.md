### Honest reading of the numbers
The benchmark runs Sentinel-2 at 10 m against 0.5 m LiDAR, so per-class raw RMSE lands between
3.1 m (hilly) and roughly 10 m (sparse and urban), with correlation strong on hilly terrain
(r = 0.99) and weak on flat sparse scenes (r = 0.02 to 0.58). This is a real result, not a failure
to hide: 10 m imagery cannot resolve single buildings. Higher-resolution scenes are the way to move
it, and the table must be presented with that caveat rather than trimmed.
